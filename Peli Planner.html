    Which case do I need?

    Every TrekPak case is test-packed with your current gear list, grid and clearance. Pick a row to switch to that case.

    The library is saved in this browser only. Use Copy library to back it up or move it to another device. Sizes are the real L × W × H of each item. Add to case puts a copy in the planner.

    Interior sizes come from Pelican's TrekPak case list. The grid is a planning grid and does not match TrekPak's own pin spacing, so check the layout against the real panels before cutting foam or buying a kit. Interior depth is the full case depth.
